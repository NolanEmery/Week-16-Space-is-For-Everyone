# Week-16-Space-is-For-Everyone
Plan It:

What is the goal of the website?

Make a website that explains how the high school STEM subjects can lead to a career in the space industry. The subjects are Math, Science, Reading & Writing, Art & Design, Engineering & Technology, and History & Teamwork. The goal of the website is to teach fifth graders about space. Use knowledge from all subjects.

Who are you building for, and what do they care about?

Fifth graders who want to play games that are simple, interactive, and educational.

Describe how a student should explore the site. What should they see first, and what happens when they pick a subject?

Use a separate page for each subject. Include buttons to click between pages. Do the challenges in order. 

What interactive activity will you design for each subject? (For example: a math quiz where the answer controls how high a spaceship flies.)

History and Teamwork (page 1): Intro to space. Science (page 2): How rockets work. Art and Design (page 3): Design your own spaceship. Engineering and Technology (page 4): Drag parts to build the spaceship (what part does). Math (page 5): Equations to show the path of a rocket. Reading (page 6): Read AI feedback.

What words best describe the look and feel that you want?

Educational, simple, fu, inspirational.

Test It:

Make it more difficult.

Expand It:

Put the activities in order. Make each activity correspond to one planet. Once they finish the activity, they unlock the corresponding planet. The planet has extra activities once unlocked.
